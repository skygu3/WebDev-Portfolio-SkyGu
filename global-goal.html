UN Global Goal

SDG 4 — Quality Education

"Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all."

— United Nations, 2015

Why I Chose This Goal

As a student learning to code, I believe access to quality education — especially in technology — is one of the most important things we can give to young people around the world.

Not everyone has the opportunity to take a course like CS12 or access to computers and the internet. This gap affects people's ability to participate in the modern economy.

How This Connects to Web Development

The web is one of the greatest tools for sharing knowledge. Free tutorials, documentation, and open-source code help people learn to build things online regardless of where they live.

By learning to build websites, I am also learning how to contribute to that ecosystem.

What Can Be Done

  • Expand free coding education in under-resourced communities
  • Ensure school computer labs have up-to-date equipment
  • Create multilingual learning resources
  • Support teachers with training in digital tools
